Sense. Think. Act.

Each telemetry frame passes through four steps. The model may only propose; hard limits decide what reaches the pumps and lights.

Ingest

Air temperature, humidity, substrate moisture, CO₂, LED temperature, reservoir level.

Derive

Vapor pressure deficit from temperature and humidity (Tetens approximation).

Propose

gpt-oss-120b receives targets and space risks, and returns a control vector with its reasoning.

Validate

Rules clamp LED power, pump and irrigation volume before actuation.

Built for Space Lead ’26

Boeing Space Lead Hackathon, Alfaisal University, 25–26 October. Three workstreams: hardware and IoT (ESP32, sensors, LED and pump actuation), LLM and biological intelligence (this dashboard’s brain), and poster and visual communication.

Limits: the dataset is synthetic (8 frames), only potato and dwarf tomato are defined, and the safety rules cover three hazards.
